I hope this message finds you well. I am writing on behalf of Dr. JooHyun Lee and myself to ask for your review of a survey proposal for KSII Transactions on Internet and Information Systems.

The proposed survey is a follow-up to our previous TIIS survey, "Generative AI Features and Limitations in Support of Inclusive XR Immersive Learning Environments for Aging and Creative Community Applications" (TIIS-S1-2025-Nov-1354.R1; vol. 20, no. 4, 2026; DOI: 10.3837/tiis.2026.04.027). The previous survey identified the limitations of generative AI in multimodal, latency-sensitive learning environments. It also proposed an evaluation model. The new survey applies those questions to one concrete domain: physical craft making.

**Proposed title:** Internet-Connected Generative AI for Human Making in Craft: A Critical Review of In-Process Feedback and Learning

**Research questions**
- RQ1: How does AI observe and represent making actions, material states, and maker intent?
- RQ2: What supports AI advice, and how does the maker decide to change, preserve, or extend a variation?
- RQ3: What changes in independent understanding, judgment, or skill do studies test after AI support?

**Method:** We will search the ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, Scopus, and Web of Science, and apply a common extraction scheme to every included study. The synthesis will be narrative and will not include a meta-analysis (SWiM). The proposal currently draws on 30 seed studies, all of which we checked by DOI.

The attached draft follows the TIIS Word template and reference format. It ends with a proposed writing roadmap. The roadmap applies only if the proposal is accepted, and it describes how we would use AI-assisted writing tools under author verification.

We would be grateful for your guidance on the following points:
1. Whether the scope fits TIIS, and any suggested changes to the section structure.
2. The page limit and submission timeline for survey papers.
3. Whether the proposed writing roadmap is acceptable, and the current TIIS policy on disclosing AI-assisted writing.
