# KSII-TIIS-2027

This repository contains working files for a survey proposal to **KSII Transactions on Internet and Information Systems (TIIS)**.

> **Internet-Connected Generative AI for Human Making in Craft: A Critical Review of In-Process Feedback and Learning**
> JooHyun Lee (Yonsei University) and Junheon Lee (KB Securities)

The proposal is a follow-up to our previous TIIS survey, *Generative AI Features and Limitations in Support of Inclusive XR Immersive Learning Environments for Aging and Creative Community Applications* (TIIS-S1-2025-Nov-1354.R1; vol. 20, no. 4, 2026; [doi:10.3837/tiis.2026.04.027](http://doi.org/10.3837/tiis.2026.04.027)). It takes the earlier survey's questions on multimodal alignment, feedback reliability, and learning evaluation and applies them to physical craft making.

## Research Questions

- **RQ1. Observation and representation.** How does AI observe and represent making actions, material states, and maker intent?
- **RQ2. Feedback and judgment.** What supports AI advice, and how does the maker decide to change, preserve, or extend a variation?
- **RQ3. Performance and learning.** What changes in independent understanding, judgment, or skill do studies test after AI support?
